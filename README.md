# Vatra.ai

**Open-source Physical AI & Edge Vision platform by Vantara In Tech.**

Vatra.ai makes real-world computer vision, spatial understanding, and sensor-to-AI pipelines lightweight, accessible, and free for developers, researchers, and industries globally.

---

## Why Vatra.ai?
Deploying Physical AI in the real world—such as workplace safety monitoring, visual quality inspection, and robotics perception—often requires heavy compute and complex hardware integration. **Vatra.ai** focuses on:
- **Edge-First Efficiency:** Lightweight vision and spatial pipelines built for low-latency inference on **NVIDIA GPUs** and **Jetson** edge devices.
- **Open & Modular:** Free-to-all core tooling designed to connect cameras, sensors, and simulation environments seamlessly.
- **Hardware-Accelerated:** Structured for optimization with **NVIDIA CUDA** and **TensorRT**.

## Planned Architecture
```text
[ Cameras / IoT / Sensors ] ---> [ Vatra Ingestion & Preprocessing ] ---> [ Edge Vision / Physical AI Engine ] ---> [ Alerts / Actuators / Sim ]
```
